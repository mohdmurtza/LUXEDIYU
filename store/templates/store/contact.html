Frequently Asked Questions

What are your shipping charges?
We offer free shipping on all orders above ₹5,000. For orders below ₹5,000, a shipping charge of ₹150 applies.
What is your return policy?
We offer a 30-day return policy on all unworn jewelry in original packaging. Customized and engraved items are non-returnable.
Do you provide a certificate of authenticity?
Yes, every diamond and gemstone purchase comes with a certificate of authenticity from authorized gemological labs.
How do I track my order?
Once your order is dispatched, you will receive an email and SMS with tracking details. You can also track it on our website in the "My Orders" section.
